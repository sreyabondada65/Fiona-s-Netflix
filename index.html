About Fiona Bakshi

“You’ve Been Watching Me”
An interactive digital identity experience. Instead of a boring questionnaire, you get to know me through the shows on my Netflix account.

Hi! I’m Fiona Bakshi, a 19-year-old Industrial Design student from Delhi, India.

Each of the 12 shows represents a real part of my life: my age, my hometown Delhi, my family's Punjabi traditions, my design studies, speaking Hindi and studying German, and how I experience spaces as a young woman.

What I Do in Design

  • Sketching and physical blue-foam prototyping
  • TRIZ systematic creative problem-solving
  • Designing everyday products that feel comfortable and easy to use
  • Trilingual communication: Hindi, English, and German
